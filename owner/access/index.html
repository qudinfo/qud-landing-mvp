FAQ

Key questions about access, observation and working with QUD.

QUD is not a trading signals service. Users do not receive instructions on when to buy or sell. Before execution, each trading decision passes a multi-layer check of dozens of risk parameters; the system then determines whether it may be executed on a live brokerage account.
QUD collects data automatically from the trading process. AI is an additional tool for research and identifying patterns. Information is processed using a consistent methodology, with defined calculation formulas, verification standards and rules for comparison with the observation history.
